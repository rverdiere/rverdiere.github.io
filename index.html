I am currently a Temporary Teaching and Research Assistant (ATER) at UFR IM2AG and Laboratoire Jean Kuntzmann (LJK). On december 2025, I completed my PhD in the AIRSEA team in INRIA Grenoble under the supervsion of Clémentine Prieur and Olivier Zahm.

My research to date has focused on:

  • Dimension reduction (both supervised and unsupervised)
  • Uncertainty quantification and sensitivity analysis
  • Approximation of probability densities (using sparse triangular transport maps)
  • Machine learning (Neural networks, Kernel methods)
